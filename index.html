Dr Ahmed Nabil Poster

Dr Ahmed Nabil Poster is a private publishing tool used only by Dr. Ahmed Nabil, a chemistry teacher in Egypt, to share his own educational short videos (chemistry lessons and study tips for secondary-school students) on his own social media accounts.

How it works

  1. Dr. Ahmed connects his own TikTok account once using Login with TikTok.
  2. Each day, a short lesson video he created is prepared in his private content sheet.
  3. He reviews the video and caption and approves it manually (via his private Telegram bot).
  4. Only after his approval, the tool uploads that video to his TikTok account using the Content Posting API.

The tool is not offered to the public, does not access any other user's account, and never posts without the account owner's explicit approval.
