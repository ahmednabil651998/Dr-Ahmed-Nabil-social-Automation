Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Dr Ahmed Nabil Poster ("the App") is a personal publishing tool operated by Dr. Ahmed Nabil for his own social media accounts only.

Information we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the connected account (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos the account owner approved.

How we use it

The information is used only to publish the account owner's own videos to his own TikTok account after his manual approval. We do not sell, share, or use it for advertising or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored privately in the owner's Google Apps Script project properties, accessible only to the owner. No data about other TikTok users is collected or stored.

Data deletion

The owner can disconnect the App at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); stored tokens are then deleted and no longer usable.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact Dr. Ahmed Nabil through his official Facebook page "Dr. Ahmed Nabil - Chemistry".
